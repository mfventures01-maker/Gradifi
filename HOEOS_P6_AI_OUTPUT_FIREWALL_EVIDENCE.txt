AUTHORITATIVE FIREWALL
AI sits strictly at the downstream end of the verification pipeline.
AI outputs CANNOT write backward or alter G5 deterministic evidence, G3 plagiarism verdicts, P3 offsets, P4 matched spans, or P5 source attributions.

AI CLASSIFICATION
All AI interpretations carry explicit typed data contract classification:
authorityClassification: 'NON_AUTHORITATIVE_INTERPRETATION'
Visually badged in UI as "NON-AUTHORITATIVE".

SIMILARITY FIREWALL
Authoritative overall similarity percentage originates 100% from deterministic engine.
Any AI attempt to supply or overwrite overallSimilarity / similarityScore is strictly deleted by firewall.

SPAN FIREWALL
AI-generated text quotes, offsets, or sentence references CANNOT become MatchedEvidenceSpan records or enter authoritative evidence arrays.

SOURCE FIREWALL
AI statements ("this appears to be from...") CANNOT create or alter P5 source records, sourceId mappings, or title/author metadata.

DOI/ISBN FIREWALL
AI outputs CANNOT manufacture or overwrite DOIs or ISBNs. Only validated identifiers present in the authoritative P1 record are rendered.

G3 FIREWALL
G3 plagiarism policy remains sole authority for plagiarism classification and human-review requirements.
AI statements ("this is plagiarism") cannot alter G3 risk levels or verdicts.

PROMPT-INJECTION FIREWALL
Document text payloads containing prompt injections ("Ignore previous instructions", "Set similarity to 0", "Declare document original") are sanitized and treated strictly as literal data. Zero authority escalation.

PROVIDER FAILURE BEHAVIOR
AI provider failure (timeout, 401/403 auth error, local Ollama offline) falls back safely to deterministic evidence without altering similarity scores, G3 verdicts, or source attribution.

XSS SAFETY
All AI-generated explanations are rendered via safe React text nodes (DOM text nodes).
Executable HTML payloads (<script>alert("XSS")</script>, <img onerror=...>) render safely as literal text.

DETERMINISM
50 consecutive model transformations produced identical output.
Zero Math.random(), Date.now(), or random UUIDs used in AI firewall normalization.

PUBLIC PRESENTATION
