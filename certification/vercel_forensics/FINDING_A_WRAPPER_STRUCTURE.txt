[1] ROOT CAUSE CLASSIFICATION

  ROOT CAUSE: IN-PLACE ESBUILD BUNDLE OVERWRITE

  The eleven files under `api/*.ts` and `api/verify/*.ts` are bundled output
  artifacts generated by an in-place build tool execution (`scratch/bundle_api.ps1`).
  
  Specifically, `scratch/bundle_api.ps1` executed:
    npx esbuild $f --bundle --platform=node --format=esm --target=node18 --external:@vercel/node --outfile=$f --allow-overwrite

  This command targeted the `api/*.ts` entry points directly, reading the source
  wrappers and overwriting them with inlined multi-megabyte esbuild output bundles.
