## 3. Problem Discovery Findings

Structured, neutral discovery interviews investigated existing anti-plagiarism workflows, recurring operational pain points, expenditure levels, and failure modes across the target institutions.

### 3.1 Incumbent Workflows & Expenditures
- **Incumbent Tool:** Turnitin is the dominant incumbent in 7 out of 8 surveyed institutions, primarily utilized at the Postgraduate School or University Library level.
- **Expenditure:** Turnitin annual licenses range from **$9,000 USD to $32,000 USD** per institution, depending on student enrollment tiers.
- **FX Crisis:** The depreciation of the Nigerian Naira (NGN) from ~₦460/$ in early 2023 to >₦1,500/$ in 2025/2026 has caused a **200% to 300% surge in effective Naira software costs**, forcing institutions to ration student access, delay renewal payments, or let subscriptions lapse entirely.

### 3.2 Documented Operational Pain Points
1. **Rationing & Gatekeeping:** Due to per-seat or per-submission license caps on USD software, undergraduate submissions (which account for 85%+ of student volume) are almost never scanned. Only final-stage Master's theses and Ph.D. dissertations receive checks, usually bottlenecked through a single library technician.
2. **False Positives on Statutory & Standard Terms:** Nigerian law and medical faculties report excessive false-positive flags on mandatory statutory citations (e.g. *Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999*, *Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020*) and standard laboratory procedures, requiring hours of manual post-filtering by supervisors.
3. **Bandwidth Fragility & Offline Inability:** Heavy cloud upload workflows consistently time out during frequent campus fiber outages, stalling scheduled departmental defense sessions.
4. **Lack of Local Academic Source Indexing:** Incumbent tools frequently miss student duplication against Nigerian university institutional repositories, local conference proceedings, and regional dissertations because these local repositories are not integrated into global paywalled publisher partnerships.

## 4. Testing the Gradifi Proposition: Buyer Reactions and Objections

Prospective buyers were walked through the verified **Submit $\rightarrow$ Verify $\rightarrow$ Inspect Evidence $\rightarrow$ Understand Results $\rightarrow$ Review Limitations** workflow established in Gate G6.

### 4.1 What Buyers Valued
- **Dual Authority Metrics:** Deans and Librarians unanimously praised the segregation of *Unique Matched Coverage* from *Highest Source Match*, noting that it immediately distinguishes a student who borrowed heavily from one paper versus a student who cited numerous distinct academic works properly.
- **Exact Coordinate Slicing:** Faculty reviewers appreciated that clicking a finding reveals the verbatim sentence and source snippet side-by-side with zero offset drift, eliminating manual page searches.
- **Local Currency (NGN) Invoicing:** Removing FX conversion risk and Central Bank Form A foreign remittance friction was cited as the single strongest immediate administrative incentive to consider a switch.
- **Cryptographic Tamper-Proof Receipts:** Quality assurance directors favored the public verification QR code, stating that it eliminates the widespread campus fraud of students Photoshopping forged Turnitin PDF percentage covers.

### 4.2 Key Objections and Institutional Hesitations
1. **LMS Integration Requirement:** Several institutions (notably private universities running Moodle or Canvas) stated they cannot deploy a standalone portal for all undergraduate coursework without a direct LTI/API submission plugin.
2. **NUC / Regulatory Endorsement:** Public university librarians emphasized that adopting a replacement integrity tool at scale is significantly accelerated if the National Universities Commission (NUC) or Committee of Vice-Chancellors (CVCNU) issues an advisory or benchmark recognition.
3. **Paraphrase Boundaries:** Two interviewees questioned whether Gradifi detects AI-generated paraphrases. Presenters adhered strictly to G6 disclosures, clarifying that Gradifi guarantees deterministic lexical overlap, while AI advisory insights provide non-authoritative review.
