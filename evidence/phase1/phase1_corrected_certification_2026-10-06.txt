8. PHASE 2 AUDIT RELATIONSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 2 independently executed the G1 extractor audit documented in:
  evidence/phase2/g1_extractor_audit.txt (Status: COMPLETE, Date: 2026-10-06)

The Phase 2 audit established three fatal defects in `officeZipAdapter.ts`:
  1. Zip Bomb Vulnerability:
     `MAX_UNCOMPRESSED_ZIP_BYTES = 100 * 1024 * 1024` is declared at line 36
     but never referenced or enforced. `fflate.unzipSync` unpacks without limit.
  2. Regex XML Parsing:
     Lines 67-82 use regex patterns rather than an XML parser, failing on entity
     decoding, namespace scopes, self-closing tags, `<w:tab/>`, `<w:br/>`,
     and `xml:space="preserve"`.
  3. Silent OCR Substitution:
     Lines 124-158 silently substitute nondeterministic Tesseract OCR text for
     DOCX payloads when XML content is < 50 characters, breaking byte-level determinism.

Boundary Separation:
  - Phase 1 establishes repository architecture and existence of components.
  - Phase 2 evaluates compliance against the G1 contract.
The Phase 2 failure findings are not retroactively inserted into Phase 1, but
confirm that Phase 1 must close with G1 compliance marked OPEN / FAIL.
