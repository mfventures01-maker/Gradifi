1.1BACKGROUND OF THE STUDY	

From the research carried out, today digital thermometers have almost entirely replaced the liquid in gas thermometer in the consumer market. Before the introduction of the digital thermometer, thermometer uses liquid or gas to measure temperature and lacked standards for readings. The first consistent standard came when mercury is being used. Daniel Gabriele A Fahrenheit created a temperature scale that could reproduce reliable temperatures.

In the 1990s scientists deemed the mercury based thermometer was risky to handle. It had to be swung sharply to be reset. Since then, mercury thermometers have been replaced in stores with digital ones. The basic principle for measuring temperature, however still remains the same. All heat meters require a temperature sensor and a means of translating a change in temperature into a numerical value for example, in a mercury thermometer the temperature sensor is the mercury. The change in numerical value registers on the scale on the thermometer.

The most common way for a digital thermometer to measure temperature is via a thermal resistor or transistor. When a change in temperature occurs the device chan ges its resistance or conductivity, then a circuit translates that resistance into a reading.
